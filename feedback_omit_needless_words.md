Cut every word that does not carry information. No section preambles, no
restating the question, no "worth noting" framing, no explaining that an
explanation follows. One idea per sentence, dropped in.

**Why:** the repo's own standard (CLAUDE.md: "Qualité dense") applies to
conversation too, not just to skill prose. David reads fast and treats
padding as noise obscuring the finding.

**How to apply:** write the reply, then delete a third of it. Prefer a
bolded lead-in plus one dense sentence over a heading plus a paragraph.
Tables beat prose for parallel facts. Keep the evidence, cut the
connective tissue.
